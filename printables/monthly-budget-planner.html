How to use it

  1. Write your take-home income in the Income box first.
  2. Fill in the Budget column for each expense before the month starts.
  3. Keep the sheet somewhere handy and add to the Actual column as bills are paid.
  4. At the end of the month, fill in the +/– column to see where you went over or saved.
  5. Use what you learn to set next month's budget.

Supplies that make this easier

  • Budget binder with cash envelopes if you like the cash envelope method
  • Desk calculator
  • 3-ring binder to keep each month's sheet together
