Free Printable Winter Break Chore Chart for Kids

Winter break is wonderful, until day three, when the house is a mess and everyone is bored. A little structure goes a long way, and kids are much happier to help when it feels like a game.

This winter break chore chart is a snowy board game. Kids fill in a circle for each chore they finish that day, then color the next square on the path. When they reach the end, they win the winter prize you write at the bottom. Winter chores like carrying boots and helping shovel are already filled in, with a blank row for your own.

How to use it

  1. Print one chart per week of break and hang it where everyone can see it.
  2. Swap any pre-filled chore for one that fits your home, or add your own in the blank row.
  3. Choose the winter prize together: sledding with a friend, a movie night, or picking the next family outing.
  4. Let your child color the circles and board squares themselves. That's the fun part.
  5. Pair it with the free Christmas Break Daily Schedule so the whole day has a rhythm.

Supplies that make this easier

  • Winter reward stickers snowflakes and snowmen for the board squares
  • Washable markers
  • Kids snow shovel so little helpers can really pitch in
