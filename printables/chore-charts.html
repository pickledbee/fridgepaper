Not sure which chores fit your child's age? Read age-appropriate chores for kids and how to make a chore chart that actually works.

5 tips to make any chore chart work

  1. Start with three to five chores your child can do alone.
  2. Hang the chart at their eye level with a marker next to it.
  3. Check it at the same time every day, like right before dinner.
  4. Agree on the reward together before the week starts.
  5. Print a fresh chart every Sunday night.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best chore chart for a 5 year old?

The kids chore chart (ages 4 to 10) works well for most 5 year olds. If your child isn't reading yet, start with the toddler chart, which uses pictures.

How many chores should a child have?

Three to five chores is a good start for most kids. Add one more after a successful week.

Are these chore charts free?

Yes. Every chart is free to download and print, with no email or sign-up.

Can I make a chore chart with my own chores?

Yes. Use the free Chore Chart Maker to type your child's name, chores and reward, then download the PDF.

Do the charts print on regular paper?

Yes. They're designed for standard US Letter paper (8.5 x 11). Choose Actual size or 100% when you print.

Should kids get paid for chores?

Many families pay for extra jobs and keep everyday chores unpaid. The allowance calculator can help you pick a fair amount.
