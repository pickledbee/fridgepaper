Free Printable Christmas Break Daily Schedule for Kids

Two weeks at home with no school schedule can turn into late mornings, too much screen time and a lot of "I'm bored." A simple daily schedule on the fridge gives kids a rhythm without taking the fun out of break.

This Christmas break schedule has 13 colorful time blocks from wake-up to bedtime, a before-screens checklist kids finish first, 10 winter boredom busters, and a spot for today's fun plan. The times are just a guide. Cross any of them out and write your own.

How to use it

  1. Look over the times and change any that don't fit your family. A pen is fine.
  2. Go over the before-screens checklist with your kids so everyone knows the deal.
  3. Let kids check off each box as they go. It gives them a sense of control.
  4. When someone says "I'm bored," point them to the boredom busters list.
  5. Use it with the Winter Break Chore Chart for the morning chores block.
