Free Printable Toddler Chore Chart (Ages 2 to 4)

Toddlers love to help. A chore chart just gives that helping a place to live. This one keeps it to five short jobs a 2, 3 or 4 year old can actually do, with big circles that are easy for small hands to color.

There's one blank row for a job of your own, and a reward line at the bottom. For this age, the reward can be as small as picking the bedtime book.

How to use it

  1. Read the chores out loud together. Show what each one looks like the first time.
  2. Do the job with your child for the first week. Toddlers learn by copying.
  3. Let your child color the circle right after the job is done, not at bedtime.
  4. Keep the reward small and quick: a sticker, a dance party, picking the book.
  5. Cross out a job that's too hard for now and write in one that fits.
