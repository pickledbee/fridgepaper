How to use it

  1. Sit down together on New Year's Day or the first weekend of January.
  2. Pick a family word first. Kids are great at this. Think "brave," "together" or "kind."
  3. Choose one small goal in each area, and write the very first step.
  4. Let everyone write their own goal and how the family will cheer them on.
  5. Fill in a fun plan for each month, then have everyone sign it and hang it on the fridge.

Supplies that make this easier

  • Washable markers so everyone can sign in their own color
  • Magnetic picture frame keeps it nice on the fridge all year
  • Kids goal journal for kids who want to track their own goal
