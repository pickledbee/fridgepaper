How to use it

  1. Keep a copy on the fridge all week and add items as you run out.
  2. Before shopping, check your meal planner and add anything missing.
  3. Write your budget at the top so you stay on track.
  4. Shop section by section in the order of the list.
  5. Not sure what your budget should be? Try the free Grocery Budget Calculator.
