Free Printable Weekly Cleaning Schedule

Spending all of Saturday cleaning is nobody's idea of fun. Splitting the work into small daily jobs keeps the house under control without the marathon.

This schedule has three columns: a short list of every-day tasks, one focus area for each day of the week, and a handful of once-a-month jobs.

How to use it

  1. Do the every-day column first. It takes about 15 to 20 minutes.
  2. Add the one-room-a-day job. Most take 20 to 30 minutes.
  3. Pick one monthly job each week so they never pile up.
  4. Check boxes as you go. It's oddly satisfying.
  5. Swap the days around to fit your family's schedule.

Supplies that make this easier

  • Microfiber cleaning cloths
  • Cordless stick vacuum makes the daily tidy faster
  • Cleaning caddy carry everything room to room
