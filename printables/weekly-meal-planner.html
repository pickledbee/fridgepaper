How to use it

  1. Pick a planning time. Sunday afternoon works for most families.
  2. Check what's already in the fridge and pantry first, and plan meals around it.
  3. Fill in dinners first. Breakfast and lunch can repeat during the week.
  4. As you write each meal, add any missing ingredients to the grocery list.
  5. Hang it on the fridge so nobody has to ask what's for dinner.

How to plan a week of meals in 20 minutes

  1. Check the calendar first. Busy nights (practices, late work) get the easiest meals: leftovers, sandwiches, a slow cooker dinner.
  2. Shop your fridge. Write down what needs to be used up and plan two meals around it.
  3. Pick theme nights. Taco Tuesday, pasta Wednesday, pizza Friday. Themes cut planning time in half.
  4. Plan one leftover night. Cook double on Monday and eat it again on Thursday.
  5. Fill in the grocery list as you go. Every time you write a dinner, add what you don't have to the grocery box on the same page.
  6. Use the prep box. Write what to do ahead on Sunday: chop vegetables, cook rice, thaw meat.

How a meal plan saves money

When you plan the week, you buy only what you'll cook, so less food gets thrown away and you make fewer "quick trips" to the store. Most families also eat out less because dinner is already decided at 5 p.m. Want to see your numbers? Try the free grocery budget calculator, then read how to lower your family's grocery bill.

Frequently asked questions

Does this meal planner include a grocery list?

Yes. There's a grocery list box and a prep-ahead box on the same page, so you plan meals and shopping in one place.

Does it cover breakfast, lunch and dinner?

Yes. All seven days have room for breakfast, lunch and dinner. Many families only fill in dinner, and that's fine.

Is the meal planner free?

Yes. It's free to download and print with no sign-up. Print a new one every week.

Can I print it in black and white?

Yes. Use the Black and white (ink saver) button above to save colored ink.

What size paper does it print on?

Standard US Letter paper (8.5 x 11). Set the scale to Actual size or 100% when you print.

How do I stick to a meal plan?

Hang it on the fridge where the whole family sees it, plan easy meals for busy nights, and keep one leftover night. A plan you can see is a plan you follow.
