Free Printable Chore Chart for Kids

Kids do better with chores when they can see them. A chart on the fridge turns "Did you do it?" into a quick look.

This chart turns chores into a board game. Kids fill in a circle for each chore they finish that day, then color the next square on the game board. When they reach the treasure chest, they win the prize written at the bottom. Six common chores are filled in, with a blank row for your own.

How to use it

  1. Pick 4 to 6 chores that fit your child's age. Younger kids do best with fewer.
  2. Cross out any pre-filled chore that doesn't fit and write your own in the blank row.
  3. Agree on the reward together before the week starts.
  4. Let your child color the circles and board squares themselves. That's the fun part.
  5. Want custom chores and names typed in? Use the free Chore Chart Maker.
