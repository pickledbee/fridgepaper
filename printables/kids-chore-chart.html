Free Printable Chore Chart for Kids

Kids do better with chores when they can see them. A chart on the fridge turns "Did you do it?" into a quick look.

This chart has six common chores already filled in and two blank rows for your own. Kids color in a star for each day they finish, and there's a line at the bottom for the reward they're working toward.

How to use it

  1. Pick 4 to 6 chores that fit your child's age. Younger kids do best with fewer.
  2. Cross out any pre-filled chore that doesn't fit and write your own in the blank rows.
  3. Agree on the reward together before the week starts.
  4. Have your child color in the star themselves. That's part of the fun.
  5. Want custom chores and names typed in? Use the free Chore Chart Maker.

Supplies that make this easier

  • Reward sticker sheets for kids who'd rather use stickers than color
  • Washable markers
  • Thermal laminator laminate it once and reuse it with dry-erase markers
