How to use it

  1. Type your child's name.
  2. List their chores, one per line (up to 7). Each one gets a matching picture.
  3. Add the treasure prize they're working toward.
  4. Check the preview, then press Print.
  5. Your chart is remembered on this device, so next week just press Print again.

Why make a custom chore chart?

Every family is different. One kid feeds the cat, another practices piano, another walks the dog. A custom chart lists exactly your child's jobs and their name at the top, so it feels like theirs. Kids are more likely to follow a chart they helped make.

How the chore chart maker works

  1. Type your child's name.
  2. Type up to seven chores, one per line. Keep them short: "Feed the dog," not "Remember to feed the dog before school."
  3. Type the reward they're working toward.
  4. Click Download PDF to save it, or Print to print it right away.

Nothing you type is saved or sent anywhere. It all happens in your browser.

Chore ideas to type in

Little kids: toys in the bin, clothes in the hamper, plate to the sink, shoes by the door, feed the pet.

School age: make my bed, brush my teeth, pack my backpack, set the table, empty the dishwasher, homework, practice my instrument.

Tweens and teens: take out trash, vacuum my room, do my laundry, cook one dinner, clean the bathroom.

Need help choosing? See age-appropriate chores for kids.

Making charts for more than one kid

Make one chart per child so nobody argues about whose square is whose. Use different colored markers for each kid, and hang the charts side by side. If siblings are close in age, give them the same number of chores so it feels fair.

Frequently asked questions

Is the chore chart maker free?

Yes. It's completely free, with no account, email or watermark.

Do you save the names I type?

No. Everything happens in your browser. Nothing you type is sent or stored.

How many chores can I add?

Up to seven chores, plus a reward. That's plenty. Most kids do best with three to five.

Can I save the chart as a PDF?

Yes. Click Download PDF to save it, then print it any time.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. You can make and download the chart on a phone, tablet or computer.
