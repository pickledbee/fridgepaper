Free Chore Chart Maker: Make a Custom Printable Chore Chart

Type your child's name and their chores, and this tool builds a printable chart for you. Make one for each kid in under a minute.

How to use it

  1. Type your child's name.
  2. List their chores, one per line (up to 12).
  3. Add the reward they're working toward.
  4. Pick a color, then press Print.
  5. Your chart is remembered on this device, so next week just press Print again.

Supplies that make this easier

  • Thermal laminator laminate the chart and reuse it all year
  • Fine-tip dry-erase markers
  • Reward sticker sheets
