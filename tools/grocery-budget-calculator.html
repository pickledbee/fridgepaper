Grocery Budget Calculator: Weekly, Per Person and Per Meal

Enter your monthly grocery budget and how many people you feed. The calculator breaks it down into a weekly amount, a daily amount per person, and what each home-cooked meal can cost.

How to use it

  1. Enter what you spend (or want to spend) on groceries each month.
  2. Enter how many people you feed.
  3. Estimate meals eaten at home. Three meals a day, every day, is 21.
  4. If your grocery trips include paper towels and cleaning supplies, enter roughly what share that is.
  5. Write the weekly number at the top of your printable grocery list.

Supplies that make this easier

  • Glass meal prep containers cooking ahead saves money
  • Small chest freezer lets you stock up when meat is on sale
  • Digital kitchen scale
