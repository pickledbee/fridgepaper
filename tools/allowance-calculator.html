Allowance Calculator: How Much Allowance by Age (Save, Spend, Give)

A popular rule of thumb is $1 a week for every year of a child's age, but every family is different. Set your own rate and split, and see what each child gets.

Spend gets whatever is left.

How to use it

  1. List each child's age, separated by commas.
  2. Choose a rate. $1 per year of age per week is a common starting point; many families use 50 cents.
  3. Decide how much goes to saving and giving. The rest is for spending.
  4. Pay on the same day every week so it becomes a habit.
  5. Tying allowance to chores? Pair it with a custom chore chart.
