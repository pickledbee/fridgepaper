About FridgePaper

FridgePaper makes free, simple printables and tools that help families plan meals, share chores, stick to a budget and keep the house under control, without apps, logins or subscriptions.

Everything is designed to print cleanly on regular letter-size paper on a home printer, and to look good hanging on the fridge.

Hi, I'm Bruce. I'm a dad of two boys and a girl, and a Suzuki violin teacher in Northeast Wisconsin, and I've spent more than 20 years helping kids build daily habits with charts and practice routines. FridgePaper is my way of sharing the charts that work, for free. FridgePaper is run under Pickled Bee Studios LLC.

Have an idea for a printable or tool you wish existed? Send it in. Reader requests are how new printables get made.
