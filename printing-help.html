How to print FridgePaper printables

Every FridgePaper printable is made for regular 8.5 x 11 inch (Letter) paper, the same paper that goes in any home printer or office copy machine. Here's how to get a perfect page every time.

The quick way

  1. 1
    On any printable, click Print. The finished page opens in a new tab.
  2. 2
    Press Ctrl + P (Windows) or Command + P (Mac), or tap the printer icon.
  3. 3
    Check that Paper size says Letter (8.5 x 11) and Scale says Actual size, 100% or Default. In Chrome, these are under "More settings."
  4. 4
    Click Print.

Here's what to look for in the print window:

"My printer or copy machine says to load 8.5 x 11 paper"

This means your computer told the printer to use a different paper size, often A4, which is the European size. The printable is fine. Change the setting once and the message goes away.

On a Windows computer

  1. Click Start, then Settings.
  2. Click Bluetooth & devices, then Printers & scanners.
  3. Click your printer or copier, then Printing preferences.
  4. Find Paper size (sometimes called Output size or Page size) and choose Letter or 8.5 x 11.
  5. If there's a Paper source or Tray setting, choose Auto or the tray with your regular paper.
  6. Click Apply, then OK.

On a Mac

  1. In the print window, click the Paper Size menu and choose US Letter.
  2. To make it permanent: open the Apple menu, then System Settings, then Printers & Scanners. Set Default paper size to US Letter.

On an iPhone or iPad

  1. Open the PDF, tap the Share button (the square with an arrow), then Print.
  2. Tap Options and make sure Paper size is Letter.

On an Android phone

  1. Open the PDF, tap the three dots, then Print.
  2. Tap the small down arrow under the printer name and set Paper size to Letter.

Still asking? Check the copy machine

Office copiers have their own tray settings. On the copier's touch screen, look for Paper or Tray settings and make sure the tray with your paper is set to 8.5 x 11 or Letter.

Other quick fixes

  • Part of the page is cut off: set Scale to Fit to page instead of Actual size.
  • It printed on 2 pages: use the Print or Download PDF button instead of your browser's print command. The buttons always make a one-page file.
  • The colors look faded: in the print window, look for Quality and choose Normal or High instead of Draft.
  • No color printer? It prints fine in black and white. Kids can color it in!
